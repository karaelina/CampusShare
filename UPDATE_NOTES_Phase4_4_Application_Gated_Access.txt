CampusShare Phase 4.4 — Application-gated provider access

- After a successful partnership form submission, the form is hidden and a status-first confirmation appears.
- The confirmation includes Track Your Partnership plus visibly disabled Provider Login and Activate Account actions.
- Track Your Partnership opens provider-login.html with the application reference prefilled and the tracking panel opened.
- For this application-specific tracking flow, login fields/buttons and activation are locked while status is Pending Review, Verification, or Rejected.
- When Admin changes the application status to Approved in the same browser's localStorage, checking status unlocks provider login and activation for that application.
- Normal direct visits to Provider Login remain usable for existing approved partners.
- Prototype limitation: localStorage only; no cross-device sync, email notification, secure identity storage, or production authentication.
- Use sample documents only.
